RIPscrip-3 has two built-in font systems to provide added flexibility for
high-end graphics applications.  These "vector" and "scalable" fonts
provide great flexibility in text display.  A powerful column system is
available for displaying formatted text.  Try clicking on the buttons to
the right to see how the column system can be used.  

The different font systems let you to display text in different
orientations, and with different kinds of effects.  For example, you can
enable dropshadowing to give your text a 3-D shadowed appearance, or you
can turn bold, italic, underline or strikethrough on (outline fonts only).
You can control both horizontal and vertical alignment of text, causing
text to be centered, left or right-aligned.  And the outline font system
even let's you control the rotation of text.  You can rotate text in one
of sixteen different possible combinations for added support for
international language display purposes, or just to get a nice effect.

Dozens of additional fonts will be available soon for distribution with
RIPscrip-3 software.  These TrueType(tm)-style fonts will greatly extend 
the available typefaces both in the "stock" editions of RIPscrip-3 
software, and in additional add-on font packs.
