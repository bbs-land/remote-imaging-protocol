# Fill Defects in the Shipping Implementation

[◀ Prev: Canvas Tiers, Palette & RGB Rendering](2.0-canvas-palette-rgb.md) · [Contents](README.md) · [Next: Drawing Composition ▶](2.3-drawing-composition.md)

This page records legacy flood-fill limitations and their evidence scope. It also preserves the withdrawal of an earlier claim that RIPtel pie/chord primitives leaked because they flood-filled an assembled boundary. Positive traces of the original handlers contradict that attribution; the [3.0 arc consumer contract](../../3.0/techspecs/2.1-arc-consumer-contract.md) documents what they actually do. The [2.0](../ripscrip/2.3-shapes-and-fills.md#rip_fill) and [3.0](../../3.0/ripscrip/2.3-shapes-and-fills.md#rip_fill) language entries point here for the separate legacy RIP_FILL discussion.

## Which generation is affected

**1.x flood fill:** RIPterm 1.5x uses Borland BGI `floodfill()`. The finite seed-stack behavior below can leave complex regions partly unfilled; its extensive corpus use does not prove every possible shape correct.

**RIPtel 3.1:** the PC Micro driver recognizes `F` with zero operands and dispatches a no-op. The 2.x executable behavior remains unverified. [Fingerprint and dispatch](../../3.0/research/issue-279-rip-fill-framing.md).

**Pie/chord attribution:** the previous `§BUG.6` manual-boundary flood-leak claim is withdrawn for the traced RIPtel 3.1 handlers, as detailed below. No equivalent 2.x-era trace is established here.

## Why RIP_FILL is documented as deprecated

The [2.0 reference](../ripscrip/2.3-shapes-and-fills.md#rip_fill) preserves the deprecated legacy syntax. The [3.0 reference](../../3.0/ripscrip/2.3-shapes-and-fills.md#rip_fill) describes the recognized opcode.

**The 2.00 Alpha 4 draft declares the command removed from the language.** Its stated reason is portability - _"fills work differently under Windows, OS/2, Macintosh, Amiga, and DOS. With all of the complexities involved, having a reliable fill operation cannot be achieved under multiple resolutions and platforms"_ `(v2.A2)` - and the 3.x documentation follows suit: `RIPSCRIP.HLP` lists no `RIP_Fill`, and the RIPtel demo corpus never floods.

The command's runtime status differs across implementations.

| Implementation | Status |
| --- | --- |
| RIPterm 1.5x | Implements it; 3,787 `!\|F` uses across 78 of the 118 scripts in the RIPtermJS 1.5x corpus |
| RIPSCRIP.DLL 3.0.7 (RIPtel 3.1) | Recognizes `F` with zero arguments and dispatches a no-op. |
| SyncTERM | Implements the 1.54 behavior deliberately for legacy scenes (`ripper.c:13593`) |
| RIPlib | Implements its own corrected flood fill |

See the [3.0 command entry](../../3.0/ripscrip/2.3-shapes-and-fills.md#rip_fill) for the original delimiter behavior.

## Summary

| Legacy flood-fill limitation | Evidence scope | Symptom | Implementation guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seed-stack drop | 1.x BGI behavior, references below | Complex regions partly unfilled | Growable seed storage and a visited set |

## §BUG.6 - withdrawn pie/chord flood-leak attribution

An earlier edition adopted RIPlib's `§BUG.6` as proof that original TeleGrafix pie/chord handlers drew an arc plus separate radial lines and then flood-filled the boundary. The original RIPtel driver contradicts that construction: I/i helpers call **GDI Pie**, _ calls **GDI Chord**, and skewed filled curves pass complete contours to **Polygon**. Native traces execute the handlers and capture those calls with borders enabled and disabled; see the [primary-source derivation](../../3.0/research/issue-14-arc-sweeps.md).

Consequently this record no longer attributes a manual boundary-gap flood leak to those original paths or generalizes it backward to 2.x. This correction does not claim every historical rasterizer free of defects. The actual recovered whole-turn and radial-closure quirks are in the [3.0 angle reference](../../3.0/ripscrip/2.2-curves.md#angle-ordering-and-sweeps), independently of exact pixel coverage.

## RIPlib's patterned flood attribution

RIPlib `§DEAD.7` attributes a solid border-color flood to the original DLL. The verified PC Micro RIPtel 3.1 route dispatches to a bare return. RIPlib's later crosswalk identifies that same inactive route. Its own two-pass fill remains a separate legacy implementation. [Original and RIPlib evidence](../../3.0/research/issue-279-rip-fill-framing.md).

## The seed-stack drop

Documented in full at [1.54](../../1.54/techspecs/2.1-pattern-fill-rasterization.md#fill-styles-and-patterns), with corpus figures and source citations in the [2.0 errata](../ripscrip/errata.md#rip_fill-declared-removed-but-still-implemented): the underlying BGI flood fill queues its seeds on a fixed-capacity stack (492 entries as RIPterm builds it) and **silently drops** seeds once it fills, so a complex region can come back partly unfilled. Artists did not avoid the command despite this - 3,787 `!|F` uses across 78 of the 118 scripts in the RIPtermJS 1.5x corpus.

## Guidance

- Implement region membership and pattern application independently for a portable legacy flood fill; growable work storage avoids silently dropping seeds. Period reproduction may deliberately retain a specific client's limitations when explicitly identified.
- Use the command-specific [3.0 angle contract](../../3.0/ripscrip/2.2-curves.md#angle-ordering-and-sweeps) for pie/chord empty, full and wrapped cases. Their topology is established by original consumers; the withdrawn manual-flood explanation is not a reason to change their wire semantics.
