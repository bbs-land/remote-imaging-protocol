- **Playback.** The local engine uses the RIP-enabled state at file entry. Both wire `1R` and HCL scene playback reach the same reader. With RIP enabled, `0x2ee0` feeds normalized bytes to the framer. Ordinary results reach the text router at `0x2f30`, and accepted commands go through ordinary dispatch, even within a `.TXT` story. The eligible article receives only the ordinary text path. The guide retains the corpus role of prose story files and updates the related mixing paragraph. [Source route](issue-179-txt-playback.md#route-and-decision) · [Scene framing](../ripscrip/5.5-file-transfer-and-queries.md#scene-framing-and-article-flow) · [PR #183](https://github.com/bbs-land/remote-imaging-protocol/pull/183).
- **Line endings.** The earlier framing capture distinguishes a bare LF from a line continuation. `!|Tleft` plus LF delivers `left` and following `right` uses the ordinary text path. A preceding continuation backslash preserves the pending text argument. CRLF remains the corpus convention and byte preservation remains the authoring advice. [Framing vectors](issue-7-framing-identification.md#distinguishing-streams-and-stages) · [Line-ending contract](../ripscrip/1.1-protocol-and-command-hierarchy.md#chaining-continuation-and-line-endings).
- **Encoded stream.** The ALPHA 4 UU encoding description is historical. Original `9U` handler `0x252c0–0x252f1` calls the pending-work service at `0x252cb`, compares the unsigned type to 1 at `0x252d3–0x252d6`, and reports a local type error for larger values. The handler returns without entering a different byte parser.
