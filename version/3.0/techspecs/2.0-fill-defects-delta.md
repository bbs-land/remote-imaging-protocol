# Fill Defects (delta)

[◀ Prev: Contents](README.md) · [Contents](README.md) · [Next: Arc Consumer Contract ▶](2.1-arc-consumer-contract.md)

The [legacy flood-fill record](../../2.0/techspecs/2.1-fill-defects.md) distinguishes the inherited seed-stack limitation, the adopted patterned-flood claim and the withdrawn manual pie/chord leak attribution. This page records the evidence specific to RIPtel 3.1 / driver 3.0.7.

- **Pie/chord source correction:** original I/i helpers invoke GDI Pie, _ invokes GDI Chord, and skewed filled variants submit Polygon contours. The [native derivation](../research/issue-14-arc-sweeps.md) contradicts this repository's earlier adoption of RIPlib `§BUG.6` as an observed arc-plus-radii flood construction. That attribution is withdrawn; it must not be generalized to earlier clients or new skewed commands merely from engine lineage.
- **Actual curve quirks:** original angle reduction, O/V separation, full-turn degeneracy and skewed pie center closure are now traced directly. The [angle reference](../ripscrip/2.2-curves.md#angle-ordering-and-sweeps) and [consumer contract](2.1-arc-consumer-contract.md) state those findings with API boundaries, separately from Windows rasterization.
- **Legacy RIP_FILL survives:** the original DLL retains its legacy border-color flood path despite the language's removal declaration and absent help/corpus uses. RIPlib `§DEAD.7` reports its patterned brush is not applied, producing a solid flood; this separate adopted claim does not describe ordinary GDI-filled pies or polygons.
- **Earlier-client scope:** 2.x behavior requires evidence from a 2.x binary. Shared resource magic and ancestry do not establish identical angle or fill defects.
